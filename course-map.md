# 2026 統計学B — curriculum map

This working map follows Tsumura's 2026 sequence and uses the matching Tsumura PowerPoint as the authoritative lecture deck for every lecture week. `materials/2026-tsumura/` is the consolidated master deck folder and contains every source PowerPoint with a matching PDF. Student AL worksheets are linked from their matching `weeks/` folder; quiz-import files and answer keys are not linked. The former 2026 standard materials are retained under `archive/2026-standard/`; Yoh's original materials remain in place under `other material/2025 yoh/`.

## Curation choices

- Week 4 has no Yoh `w4.pdf` in the supplied material. Tsumura's active-learning deck is the lecture reference, while Yoh's discrete-distribution active-learning worksheet remains supplementary.
- The broad Yoh continuous-distribution deck is not linked as a whole. Week 05 uses the Yoh materials that match density, normal distribution, and moments; Week 06 uses the materials for sampling distributions, the central limit theorem, t, and chi-square.
- The original Yoh #09 PDF is preserved untouched. Its W08 and W09 page ranges are copied into `materials/2026-yoh-supplements/` so students receive only the matching section.
- Yoh #02 and #12 remain intact and now accompany Tsumura's Week 12 and Week 13 regression decks respectively.
- Each Tsumura PowerPoint has a matching PDF in the master deck folder. Students download the AL worksheet for class and enter the requested cell values in the UNIPA quiz; quiz-upload files and answer keys are not linked from the agenda.
- Removing links does not make answer files private on a static site. Keep the downloaded `2026-津村ベース版` source folder and existing answer files out of any public deployment.
