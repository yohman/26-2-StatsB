## Curation choices

- The original Week 4 was absent: no Yoh `w4.pdf` is present in the supplied material. Its 2026 activity week is reconstructed from Yoh's discrete-distribution active-learning worksheet, with the 2026 Standard deck retained as the lecture reference.
- Yoh's former continuous-distribution week was a single broad lecture. Its normal/CDF/CLT content now belongs in Week 05, while t and chi-square material is placed in Week 06; the activity follows in Week 07.
- Yoh's former early regression lecture is intentionally moved to Week 12, where the 2026 Standard places it. The later Yoh interpretation lecture is paired with it.
- Slide images were not copied: the selected PDFs preserve them. Worksheets, source data, tables, and notebooks that students may use were retained.
