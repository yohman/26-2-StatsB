CORE FORMULAS & CONCEPTS

W01 · PROBABILITY

0 ≤ Pr(A) ≤ 1
Pr(Ω) = 1

Probability is bounded between zero and one; the entire sample space has probability one.

W02–04 · BINOMIAL

Pr(X=x) = ⁿCₓ πˣ(1−π)ⁿ⁻ˣ

For repeated Bernoulli trials: choose x successes, then combine success and non-success probabilities.

W03 · EXPECTATION & VARIANCE

E(X)=nπ
Var(X)=nπ(1−π)

For a binomial distribution. In the Standard deck, the Poisson approximation has E(X)=Var(X)=nπ.

W05 · STANDARDISATION

Z = (X−μ) / σ

Transform a normal observation to the standard normal scale. The central limit theorem connects this to sample means.

W06–07 · UNBIASED VARIANCE

s² = Σ(Xᵢ−X̄)² / (n−1)

Use degrees of freedom n−1 when calculating the sample unbiased variance.

W08 · POINT ESTIMATION

X̄ = (1/n)Σxᵢ
E(X̄)=μ

The sample mean is an unbiased estimator of the population mean.

W09 · MEAN CONFIDENCE INTERVAL

X̄ ± tα,n−1 √(s²/n)

Interval estimation for a population mean uses the t distribution and the sample unbiased variance in the Standard examples.

W10 · CONFIDENCE

wider interval
→ more confidence

In the screw-length activity, identify the known quantities first: population variance, sample size, and sample mean.

W11 · HYPOTHESIS TEST

t = (X̄−μ) / √(s²/n)

Set a null hypothesis, select a significance level, calculate the statistic, and compare with the rejection region / critical value.

W12 · REGRESSION

ŷ = a + bx

Interpret the intercept a and regression coefficient b. A relationship is not by itself evidence of causation.
